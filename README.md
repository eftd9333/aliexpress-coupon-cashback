## Coupon versus cashback: which one is better?

The answer depends on the cart.

Suppose your eligible item subtotal is $100. A $14 coupon reduces the amount immediately. A 3% cashback rate would produce approximately $3 before exclusions and caps, and that reward may take weeks to confirm. In that example, the immediate coupon is plainly more valuable.

Cashback becomes more interesting when:

- The order qualifies for a ShopBack-featured coupon.
- The product is eligible.
- Shipping and taxes are not a large part of the final bill.
- You already planned to buy the item.
- The cashback cap does not materially reduce the reward.
- You are comfortable waiting for confirmation.

The word **eligible** is doing a lot of work here. ShopBack calculates cashback on qualifying merchandise, not necessarily the full amount charged to your card. Shipping, taxes, and service charges are excluded. A $100 checkout total may contain only $78 of eligible merchandise after those amounts are removed. Applying 3% to the full $100 would overestimate the reward.

For a small order, a direct coupon usually wins. For a larger order, compare the coupon and cashback terms together. If the coupon is not listed by ShopBack, using it may save money immediately but eliminate the cashback claim. That can still be the right choice; the better option is the one that produces the lower final cost, not the one with more labels attached to it.

## What to check before ordering from AliExpress

A coupon is only one part of the purchase decision. AliExpress is a marketplace, so the same product name can appear across different sellers with different specifications, delivery routes, return terms, and included accessories. ShopBack’s own shopping guide recommends comparing the exact listing rather than relying only on a product title or discount badge.

Before paying, check:

### Seller and listing quality

Look at the seller’s rating, order count, review volume, and recent customer photos. For electronics, compare the exact model number, connector, voltage, storage capacity, or supported protocol. For clothing, check the seller’s measurements rather than assuming US sizing. For replacement parts, compare dimensions and compatibility details.

### Shipping and warehouse

A low item price can become less attractive after shipping. Check whether the listing ships from China, a US warehouse, or another location. Delivery estimates are listing-specific, not marketplace-wide guarantees. If you need the item by a fixed date, a slightly higher local-warehouse price may be more sensible than the cheapest cross-border listing.

### Returns and buyer protection

Read the return policy attached to the actual listing. Keep the order details, tracking information, photos, and packaging for higher-value purchases. A refund or return can also remove ShopBack cashback, so do not treat a pending reward as final money until the order has cleared the confirmation period.

### Final checkout amount

Apply the AliExpress coupon first, then inspect shipping, tax, and any service fees. Check the selected variant again before payment. Marketplace checkout pages are very good at letting a different bundle quietly replace the one you thought you selected.
